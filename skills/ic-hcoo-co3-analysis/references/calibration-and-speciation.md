# IC calibration and speciation

Keep the standard-preparation table separate from integrated peak areas. Fit each analyte only inside its validated response range, retain the intercept and residuals, and apply sample dilution and blanks after integration. Reinspect the raw chromatogram when retention shifts or peaks overlap.

The carbonate-channel peak can reflect carbonate/bicarbonate chemistry set by eluent and sample pH. Report the measured analytical species or total inorganic carbon exactly as calibrated. A conversion to another species requires an explicit equilibrium or preparation assumption; it is not a file-format property.
