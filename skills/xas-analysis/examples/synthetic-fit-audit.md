# Synthetic EXAFS fit audit

This is an invented QC illustration, not measured XAS or a structural assignment.

Suppose a proposed fit uses `k=3–10 Å⁻¹` and `R=1–3 Å`. The independent-point estimate is `2 × 7 × 2 / π ≈ 8.9`. If the model floats 10 independent variables, it is underdetermined even if its plotted residual appears small. Reduce or constrain parameters, inspect correlations, and compare physically plausible alternative paths before assigning coordination.

The report should include energy alignment, normalization, `Rbkg`, transform windows, k weights, model paths, `S0²` source, independent-parameter count, uncertainties, and both raw and fitted-window diagnostics.
