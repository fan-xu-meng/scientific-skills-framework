# BET fit-range QC

Pair sample and report files by stable identifiers, not filename order. Verify adsorption branch, units, relative-pressure range, linearized BET points, slope, intercept, and monolayer capacity. The fitted interval must be reported and inspected; a high regression score alone does not validate the BET assumptions or the chosen range.

Keep the instrument-reported area beside the independently reproduced fit. Label Langmuir, t-plot, and BJH outputs with their own methods and units. Missing or inconsistent SMP/REP pairs are QC holds, not zero-valued results.
