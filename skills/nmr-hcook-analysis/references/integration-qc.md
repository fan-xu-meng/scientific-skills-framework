# Quantitative NMR integration QC

Save the acquisition parameters, processed spectrum, phase/baseline method, chemical-shift reference, integration window, and whether the target peak overlaps an impurity. Use the same method for standards and samples. Record any internal-standard mass or concentration and its uncertainty.

The peak area is not a concentration until mapped through a matrix-matched calibration or validated response factor. Do not compare independently normalized spectra as though their peak areas were absolute. Flag receiver-gain changes, insufficient relaxation, saturation, and spectra with unreliable phase or baseline.
